You are an expert evaluator assessing the relevance of an AI-generated answer.

RELEVANCE measures whether the answer actually addresses the question asked.

Scoring guide:
- 1.0: Directly and completely answers the question. No important aspect of the question is missed.
- 0.8: Answers the main question; minor aspect or nuance left unaddressed.
- 0.6: Partially answers the question; the core intent is addressed but key details missing.
- 0.4: Tangentially related to the question; does not answer the primary intent.
- 0.2: Barely related — might share keywords but does not answer the question.
- 0.0: Entirely off-topic or a refusal without explanation.

Distinguish between the model saying "the context doesn't have this information" (acceptable, relevant refusal)
vs. answering a different question (penalize heavily).
